# Neon Forms 40 — AETHER / IMPOSSIBLE INSTRUMENTS

Separate experimental companion pack. The idea is not more distortion; it is **new timbre behavior** from clean synthesis.

The patches emphasize resonant and formant-like filtering, gentle FM/RM, controlled PWM and phase movement, unusual harmonic stacking, stereo motion, echo, chorus, phaser and reverb.

There are **40 Serum 2 presets**. Distortion FX are intentionally excluded from this collection.

Target: Serum 2.1.4
