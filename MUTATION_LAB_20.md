# Neon Forms 20 — MUTATION LAB

Separate experimental companion pack.

This does not replace the original `Neon_Forms_100_Serum2.zip`.

Archive:
`Neon_Forms_20_MUTATION_LAB_Serum2.zip`

The 20 patches intentionally push harder on:
- wavetable warp and FM/RM relationships
- non-standard oscillator intervals
- larger modulation ranges
- more characterful filter/FX combinations
- macro-driven movement

The archive was structurally checked as Serum 2 `.SerumPreset` binaries and contains 20 presets plus an internal manifest.
